# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place to work on, whatever your background or experience.

**Do:** be respectful, assume good intent, give and take feedback kindly, and focus on the work.

**Don't:** harass, insult, or discriminate against anyone, publish other people's private information, or keep
pushing after someone asks you to stop.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules, and may ban people who do so
repeatedly. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub. Reports are handled in confidence.

This applies to the repository, issues, pull requests and any space where people represent the project.
